Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about JavaBank Pay security, limits and UPI rules.

How secure is JavaBank Pay?

Passwords and UPI PINs are hashed with PBKDF2 and never stored in plain text. Sessions are signed tokens that expire, every payment needs your UPI PIN, and five wrong PIN tries lock it for 15 minutes.

Can I send money to non-JavaBank UPI IDs?

Not in this simulator. You can pay only other JavaBank Pay users, because there is no real UPI network behind it.

What are the daily transaction limits?

₹1,00,000 per payment and ₹1,00,000 per day. Simulated top-ups are capped at ₹10,000 at a time.

What if a payment is sent by mistake?

You can undo a payment for 30 seconds after sending it, as long as the receiver has not spent the money. Use the Undo button in History or on the confirmation card.
